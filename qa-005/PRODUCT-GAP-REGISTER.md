| PG-009 | Prometheus scrape-token configuration discoverability | The served runtime requires `TANPOPO_METRICS_SCRAPE_TOKEN` to authorize its internal Prometheus endpoint, but `serve --help` and the searched operator documentation do not expose the variable or a setup example.
