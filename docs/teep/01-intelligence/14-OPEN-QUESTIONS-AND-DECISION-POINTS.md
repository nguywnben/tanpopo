| **`OQ-09`** | **Policy-as-Code Engine Selection (`Cedar` vs `CEL` vs `OPA`, `DOM-10`)** | **A (`Native Stage 1` + `cedar-policy/cedar-go` Stage 2 + `cel-go` Route Matchers):** Pure Go, `Apache-2.0`, non-Turing-complete, `forbid > permit` hierarchy.<br/>**B (`OPA Rego` Embedded):** Rego DSL in Core. | **Candidate Recommendation for `TEEP-03` Evaluation (Option A):** `forbid > permit` aligns naturally with Tanpopo's monotonic parent-ceiling model without Turing-complete policy loops. | `CAP-026`, `CAP-071` |
