# 05 — Enterprise Capability & Progressive Deployment Matrix

- **Document ID:** `TEEP-02A-DOC-05`
- **Purpose:** Distinguishes **Essential Self-Hosted & Enterprise Core** capabilities from **Essential Enterprise Organizational Governance**, **Optional Ecosystem Integrations**, and **Long-Term Expansion Opportunities** — verifying that solo self-hosters (`PER-01`) experience zero unnecessary operational burden while large enterprises (`PER-03`..`PER-10`) gain substantive multi-tenant, identity, financial, compliance, and fleet controls.

---

## 1. Enterprise Classification Distribution Across `FEAT-001` .. `FEAT-153`

| Enterprise Classification Tier | Feature Count | Operational Meaning for Self-Hosted vs Enterprise Adopters |
|---|---:|---|
| **`ESSENTIAL_SELF_HOSTED_AND_ENTERPRISE`** | `79` | Core capabilities valuable on Day 1 for solo/team self-hosters AND foundational for enterprises (guided setup, native protocols, model discovery, budgets, baseline PII guardrails, Console/CLI parity, Email/Telegram/In-App alerts, Update Center). Zero extra daemons required. |
| **`ESSENTIAL_ENTERPRISE_GOVERNANCE`** | `54` | Substantive multi-org, workforce IAM (`SAML`/`SCIM`), workload identity (`WIF`), `ABAC`, Four-Eyes, `FOCUS 1.1` chargeback, Cloud KMS, `GDPR` salt-shredding, What-If/Blast-Radius, Air-Gap `.tpbundle`, and Multi-Instance Fleet governance — dormant/invisible until enabled by enterprise admins. |
| **`OPTIONAL_ECOSYSTEM_INTEGRATION`** | `17` | Pluggable adapters, external neural guardrail connectors, analytical warehouse/SIEM sinks, OpenAPI-to-MCP connectors, Terraform/K8s tooling, and Marketplace packages installed only by teams using those ecosystems. |
| **`LONG_TERM_EXPANSION_OPPORTUNITY`** | `3` | Specialized advanced capabilities (`SEC 17a-4` dual-custody legal-hold archiver, internal RAG subrequests, CI OIDC red-team gates, advanced realtime/chaos extensions) staged cleanly after core platform stabilization. |
